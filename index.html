👋 Welcome to the Pumping Unit Optimization & Balancing Tool

Optimize your surface pumping unit's efficiency by calculating current gearbox loading and determining the exact counterweight adjustments needed for perfect balance.

Two Ways to Analyze:

  • 🔧 Full Analysis — the complete, dynamometer-based workflow. Upload a .dat or .dyn file (auto-detected), select the unit and stroke, enter the existing counterweight setup, and get a full 360° gearbox torque analysis: current vs. optimized loading, permissible-load boundaries, cyclic load factor, motor power, and the precise counterweight movement needed for balance.
  • ⚡ Quick CBE Estimator — a fast, standalone estimate for when a full dynamometer card isn't available. Enter only the Peak and Minimum Polished Rod Loads along with the unit size and stroke length, and get the equivalent counterbalance moment and recommended counterweight movement, based on the standard industry approximation (Effective CBE referenced through the unit's maximum torque factor).
